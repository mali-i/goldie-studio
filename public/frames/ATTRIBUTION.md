# Device frame attribution

`17-pro-blue.png`, `17-pro-orange.png` and `17-pro-silver.png` are derived from
[iPhone 17 & 17 Pro Device Frames](https://www.figma.com/community/file/1600584030566600974/iphone-17-17-pro-device-frames)
by [Kelly Hu](https://www.figma.com/@kellyhu2), licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The bezel art was exported from that file and cropped to a transparent screen
cutout.

`macbook-air.png` is sourced from
[Free MacBook Air M2 Frontview Mockup](https://www.figma.com/community/file/1210198565936992993/free-macbook-air-m2-frontview-mockup?q_id=1175958b-c39b-4ebb-b295-3fecf7c01a5d)
on Figma Community.
